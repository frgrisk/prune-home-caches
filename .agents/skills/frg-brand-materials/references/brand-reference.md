## Product portfolio

### VOR Risk Intelligence Suite

**VOR** stands for "Visualization of Risk." The VOR Risk Intelligence Suite is the umbrella platform. Position it as a hosted performance, risk, and reporting solution designed for asset owners and managers.

VOR Intelligence is a portfolio-level dashboard that enables the end-to-end analysis of their portfolio. Operated as a standalone feature or part of a package for the most comprehensive picture.

#### VOR Stream

The core platform — single solution packaging for all VOR modules. VOR Stream manages the data processing of all VOR modules. When discussing VOR broadly or the overall platform, VOR Stream is usually what's being referenced.

#### VOR Performance

Visualize performance analytics using production reports. For asset managers who need clear performance attribution and measurement.

#### VOR Reporting

Gives users a window into their portfolio — breakout and timespan control. Flexible reporting capabilities for regulatory and client-facing needs.

#### VOR Risk Premia

Risk premia analysis and decomposition. Helps portfolio managers understand the risk factors driving returns.

#### VOR Private Capital Forecasting

Cash flow modeling for private capital investments. Models future cash flows for private equity, private debt, and other alternative investments.

#### VOR Pacing Optimization

Forecast and optimize how and when to invest across intervals. Determines the optimal pace of capital deployment.

#### VOR Scenario Builder

Flexible tools to build and test scenarios for forecasting, stress testing, and what-if analysis. Enables risk managers to model market scenarios and their portfolio impact.

### CECL

Current Expected Credit Loss — FRG's solution for CECL compliance. This is a separate product line from VOR, focused on credit loss estimation under the CECL accounting standard.

## Target audiences

FRG targets three distinct buyer personas at financial institutions:

### Tech Evaluator

**Roles**: Architect (Business or Technology), VP Technology, IT Manager
**What they care about**: Cloud-native architecture, scalability, security, integration with existing systems, modern technology stack, API capabilities, deployment flexibility
**How to speak to them**: Lead with technical differentiation. Emphasize SaaS delivery, cloud-native design, and how VOR integrates with their existing infrastructure. Be specific about capabilities.

### End User

**Roles**: Analyst (Model/Risk), Risk Manager, Model Analyst
**What they care about**: Ease of use, speed of insights, accuracy, visualization quality, flexibility in reporting, time savings
**How to speak to them**: Lead with the experience. Show how VOR makes their daily work faster and more insightful. Emphasize real-time capabilities and the quality of the analytics. Use screenshots and demonstrations.

### Economic Buyer

**Roles**: Finance & Risk leadership, CRO, CFO, SVP Stress Testing
**What they care about**: ROI, total cost of ownership, compliance coverage, risk reduction, operational efficiency, vendor stability, references
**How to speak to them**: Lead with business outcomes. Frame everything in terms of risk reduction, efficiency gains, and compliance. Use the SaaS model as a cost advantage (no infrastructure, faster time to value). Include social proof and customer counts where available.

## Color system details

### Primary palette

Black and White are the foundation. They should dominate all materials.

### Color meanings (from FRG's brand archetypes)

- **White**: honest, pure, clean, pristine, bright, clear
- **Gray**: compromise, conservative, practical, neutral
- **Black**: honest, pure, clean, pristine, bright, clear
- **Green** (Lime/Forest): quality, growth, health, balance, fresh
- **Blue**: trust, loyalty, security, calm, soothing
- **Magenta**: character, kindness, uplifting, support, warm

### CMYK values (for print)

| Color        | CMYK             |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Black        | C60 M40 Y40 K100 |
| Lime         | C40 M0 Y95 K0    |
| Forest Green | C27 M0 Y21 K73   |
| Magenta      | C0 M53 Y19 K26   |
| Maroon       | C0 M28 Y15 K71   |
| Mint         | C6 M0 Y2 K8      |
| Blue         | C26 M11 Y0 K60   |

### Sub-brand color rules

**FRG (parent brand)**: Uses the full color palette — all colors are available.

**VOR Risk Intelligence Suite / VOR Stream**: Primarily Black + White + Lime. Forest Green, Mint, Blue, and Grays as supporting colors. Lime is the dominant accent — use it prominently. Do NOT use Magenta or Maroon in VOR materials.

**FRG Services**: Primarily Black + White + Magenta. Maroon and Grays as supporting colors. Do NOT use Lime, Forest Green, or Mint in Services-only materials.

## Typography details

### Chronicle Text (primary serif)

- **Designer**: Hoefler & Co
- **Use for**: Primary headings (H1), key statistics, testimonials, pull quotes
- **Styles**: Roman (regular), Italic, Roman (colored in lime for emphasis)
- **Fallback**: Libre Baskerville (Google Fonts, freely available — included in brand assets)
- **Emergency fallback**: Georgia

### Akkurat (primary sans-serif)

- **Designer**: Laurenz Brunner / Lineto
- **Use for**: Secondary headings (H2), body copy, labels, navigation, UI elements
- **Styles**: Light, Regular, Bold
- **Fallback**: IBM Plex Sans (Google Fonts, freely available — included in brand assets)
- **Emergency fallback**: Arial
